The question

Which products, retailers, regions and sales methods bring in the most revenue, and which ones earn the best profit? The goal was to give a sales team clear places to focus.

Sales more than tripled from 2020 to 2021

Sales rose from $182.1M in 2020 to $717.8M in 2021, and units sold grew from 462,000 to 2.02M.

Top retailers

West Gear and Foot Locker together account for about half of all sales. Sports Direct sells less but keeps the most profit per dollar, at a 40.7% margin.

Regions: the West sells most, the South earns most per dollar

The West brings in 30% of sales but has the lowest margin (33.2%). The South is smaller but has the highest margin (42.3%).

Products

Men's Street Footwear is the top category at 23% of sales. Men's Athletic Footwear has the lowest margin (33.7%).

Sales methods

In-store leads on volume, but online earns the best margin.

What the data suggests

  • Look at growing the South: it has the best margin (42.3%) and the second-smallest sales base.
  • Look at expanding online: it has a 39% margin but only 27.5% of sales.
  • Review pricing or costs for Men's Athletic Footwear, which has the lowest margin of any category.
  • Work more closely with Sports Direct, the most profitable retailer per dollar sold.

How I did it

  • Started from a raw sales table of 9,648 rows (retailer, region, state, city, product, price, units, sales, profit, margin, sales method, invoice date).
  • Built PivotTables for products, regions, retailers, sales method, monthly trend, year-on-year growth and top product line per region.
  • Turned them into PivotCharts with slicers on one dashboard sheet, with headline totals for sales, profit and units.
